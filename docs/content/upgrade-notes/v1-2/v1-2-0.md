## What's new

- **Running containers**: a check of every container's packages and libraries, and an option to scan new containers as they start.
- **Alpine Linux** 3.18 and later.

## Before you upgrade

- The Running containers check needs agents 1.2.0 or later: upgrade the server, then the agents.
