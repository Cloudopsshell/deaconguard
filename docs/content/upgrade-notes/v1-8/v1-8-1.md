## What's new

- **End-of-life releases in containers and images**: a container or image on a release that no longer gets fixes, such as Debian 10 or Alpine before 3.18, gets a high finding, `DG-EOL-001`, with what to rebuild on.
- The Running containers check is **partial**, not complete, when packages or libraries couldn't be checked, such as when the advisory feeds can't be reached.

## Before you upgrade

- Image scans in pipelines with `--fail-on high` (or lower) now fail on an image whose release has ended. Rebuild such images on a supported release, or expect the pipeline to stop.
