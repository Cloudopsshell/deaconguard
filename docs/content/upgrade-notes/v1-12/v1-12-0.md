Released 2026-10-29. Everything in the release: [changelog](https://github.com/Cloudopsshell/deaconguard/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#1120---2026-10-29). How to upgrade: [Upgrade](../../installation/upgrade.md).

## What's new

- **Browse a registry and watch a repository:** list a registry's repositories and tags, and scan each new push whose tag matches a pattern. See [Watch a repository](../../usage/images.md#watch-a-repository).
- **Reports of new pushes:** the new **Image scan reports** notification event sends what each new push's scan found, and what changed since the previous build, to the channels you choose. See [Reports of new pushes](../../usage/images.md#reports-of-new-pushes).
- **A page for each watched repository**, with its builds and a chart of vulnerabilities by build; the Images page groups, searches and pages its list.
- **Check a registry login** with **Check**, and saving a login now clears its registry's "Can't check for new builds" message at once.

## Before you upgrade

- The database moves to schema 27 on the first start. [Back up](../../usage/backup-and-restore.md) first: an earlier release can't open it afterwards.
- To browse or watch Amazon ECR repositories, the IAM user or role of the registry login also needs `ecr:DescribeRepositories` and `ecr:DescribeImages`. See [the IAM policy](../../usage/images.md#an-iam-policy-for-amazon-ecr). Scanning single images works without them, as before.
- Existing notification channels don't get the new **Image scan reports** event until you tick it on the Notifications page.

## Kubernetes (Helm and Kustomize)

- Upgrade as on [Upgrade](../../installation/upgrade.md#helm). The chart doesn't change.
