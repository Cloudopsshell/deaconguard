## What's new

- **A quieter access log on Kubernetes:** Kubernetes' health checks (requests from `kube-probe`) are left out of the access log unless they fail, as agents' and the dashboard's routine polls are. They added about eight lines a minute to `kubectl logs`. See [Logs](../../usage/dashboard.md#logs).
