Released 2026-10-20. Everything in the release: [changelog](https://github.com/Cloudopsshell/deaconguard/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#160---2026-10-20). How to upgrade: [Upgrade](../../installation/upgrade.md).

## What's new

- **TLS checks** (`deaconguard tls check`) and **network scans** (`deaconguard net scan`), and a **Network** page that scans targets on a schedule, with a new notification event, `exposure`.

## Before you upgrade

- The database moves to schema 24 on the first start. [Back up](../../usage/backup-and-restore.md) first: an earlier release can't open it afterwards.
